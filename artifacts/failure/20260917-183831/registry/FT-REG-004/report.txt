FT-REG-004
Result: PASS

Scenario:
The Harbor registry certificate is signed by a private CA which is not
trusted by the Kyverno admission controller.

Baseline:
The Harbor private CA was present in the Kyverno custom CA bundle and
the signed test image was successfully processed by the
verify-signed-images policy.

Failure Injection:
Replaced the Kyverno custom CA bundle with a valid test CA which does
not trust the Harbor certificate, then restarted the Kyverno admission
controller so the modified trust bundle was loaded.

Failure Verification:
DNS resolution remained healthy and harbor-public resolved to
171.244.199.186.

TCP and TLS connectivity to Harbor remained available and the registry
returned HTTP 401 for an unauthenticated request.

The Harbor certificate itself remained unchanged:
Subject: CN=harbor-public
Issuer: CN=harbor-ca
Valid until: Sep 5 2027

Runtime Result:
Kyverno attempted image verification against the Harbor registry and
failed with:

tls: failed to verify certificate:
x509: certificate signed by unknown authority

The admission request completed with a policy warning. Because the
development ImageValidatingPolicy uses Audit and Warn validation
actions, the request was admitted rather than denied.

Recovery:
Restored the original Kyverno custom CA bundle and restarted the
admission controller.

Signed-image verification succeeded again without an x509 or TLS trust
error.

Result:
PASS
