FT-RBAC-008
Result: PASS

Scenario:
The ServiceAccount binding required by the Kyverno background controller
is removed.

Baseline Verification:
The kyverno-background-controller ServiceAccount was bound to the
kyverno:background-controller ClusterRole through the
kyverno:background-controller ClusterRoleBinding.

The controller had the required read and write permissions, and the
generate-resource-quota policy successfully generated a ResourceQuota
for the baseline Namespace.

Failure Injection:
Removed the kyverno:background-controller ClusterRoleBinding while
leaving the ClusterRole itself unchanged.

Observed Result:
The target ClusterRoleBinding was successfully removed.

The background controller retained get/list/watch access to ResourceQuota
and UpdateRequest resources through its remaining view binding, but lost
create/update/patch/delete permissions provided by the removed binding.

A new Namespace matching the generate-resource-quota policy was then
created. The Namespace itself was successfully created, but the expected
namespace-resource-quota resource was not generated.

Controller logs confirmed RBAC authorization failures. The background
controller was denied permission to create ResourceQuota resources and
also lost permissions required for UpdateRequest status updates, report
creation, event creation, and other controller operations.

Recovery:
Restored the original kyverno:background-controller ClusterRoleBinding
from the baseline backup.

Post-recovery verification confirmed that the binding was present and
the required ResourceQuota and UpdateRequest permissions were restored.

Automatic retry of the previously failed generation was not observed
during the recovery observation window. The Namespace was therefore
updated to trigger a new policy evaluation.

Recovery Verification:
After the Namespace update, Kyverno processed a new UpdateRequest and
successfully generated namespace-resource-quota in the target Namespace.

Result:
PASS
