FT-RBAC-003
Result: N/A – Permission not owned by target controller

Scenario:
Background controller lacks permission to update PolicyReport resources.

Baseline Verification:
The kyverno-background-controller ServiceAccount has only
get/list/watch permissions on PolicyReport and ClusterPolicyReport
resources. It does not have create/update/patch permissions.

The kyverno-reports-controller ServiceAccount owns the report
management permissions, including create/update/patch/delete on
PolicyReport, ClusterPolicyReport, and their status subresources.

Failure Injection:
Not performed because the target permission is not granted to the
background-controller in the baseline configuration.

Conclusion:
The test assumption does not match the RBAC responsibility model of
the deployed Kyverno version. Policy report updates are handled by
the reports-controller rather than the background-controller.
