FT-RBAC-002
Result: N/A – Controller responsibility differs from test assumption

Scenario:
Background controller lacks get/list/watch permission on workload resources.

Failure Injection:
Removed the kyverno:background-controller:view ClusterRoleBinding.

Observed Result:
The background-controller ServiceAccount successfully lost Pod read
permissions (get pods = no). The controller itself remained healthy.

However, background policy evaluation continued and a PolicyReport was
successfully generated for the existing test Pod while the RBAC fault
was active.

Further verification showed that the dedicated reports-controller
ServiceAccount retained get/list/watch permissions on Pods through its
own view ClusterRoleBinding.

Conclusion:
In the deployed Kyverno architecture, removing workload read permission
from the background-controller does not reproduce the expected failure
of background policy reporting. The test assumption does not match the
current controller responsibility separation.

Recovery:
Restored kyverno:background-controller:view and verified
get/list/watch pods = yes.

Result:
N/A
